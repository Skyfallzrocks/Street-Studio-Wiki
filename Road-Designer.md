# Road designer

## Pieces and categories

Use the search box or category tabs to find sections. Available pieces depend on the loaded game content and the network being edited.

Common section types include car and bus lanes, bike sections, sidewalks, parking, medians, shoulders and track sections.

Drag a piece from the library into the bottom street layout. Reorder existing pieces by dragging them. Click a piece to open its options; use **Duplicate lane** or **Remove lane** where available.

## Direction and width

Direction is a property of the selected section. Inspect the layout arrows before placing the road, particularly with one-way roads and bike paths. Two-way direction is available only on compatible pieces.

The reported street width comes from its component sections. Available widths and combinations depend on the underlying network pieces; the designer does not guarantee that an arbitrary narrow cross-section can contain every requested lane.

For protected bike layouts, reserve space for the bike section and its separator. Check the finished junction as well as the straight segment. Bollards, painted lines and green surfacing do not by themselves establish a safe traffic connection.

## Street style

The preset workflow includes City Standard, Bus Corridor, Bike Friendly, Boulevard, Main Street, Transit Avenue and Green Corridor recipes.

Choose a recipe, adjust its available options and press **Apply Design**. A recipe replaces the cross-section. Its controls are not a live description of every possible hand-edited layout.

## Feature cards

1. Select a category such as medians, crossings, planting or parking.
2. Hold and drag a feature card onto the road or a compatible layout piece.
3. Release to apply it.
4. Press **Escape** to cancel a drag.

Features require compatible target sections. A missing feature or rejected drop can mean that the selected piece does not support it.

## Editing scope

Read the scope shown beneath the road name. Editing a design shared by multiple segments affects those segments together. Use **Copy design** when one street should develop independently.

## History

- **Undo:** Ctrl+Z.
- **Redo:** Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z.
- **Cancel a drag:** Escape.

Undo history is an editing-session feature, not a substitute for a save backup. Whole-city and whole-road-type conversions have separate irreversible warnings.
